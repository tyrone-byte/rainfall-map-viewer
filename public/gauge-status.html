Flags are based on the last valid observation timestamp, not the size of a rise or fall. Zero rainfall is valid. Repeated downloads of the same timestamp do not make a gauge current. Here, valid means a usable numeric reading; it is not a certification of BoM quality codes.

“Out of action” means no recent valid observation under these display rules; it does not confirm equipment failure or an official BoM status. Manual and event-driven gauges can legitimately report less often. When collection is stale or unavailable, gauge condition is marked unverified.
