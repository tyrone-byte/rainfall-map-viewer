# GaugeView Dams

The Dams page shows dam attributes, its water-plan area and named watercourses, and rainfall/water-level map points inside the selected area, opening the **nearest mapped water-level station** by default. Rainfall and Water levels remain separate full viewers. No collector, reading schema, retention or database content changes.

## Supplied dam attributes

49 dam/storage records, 25 Seqwater and 24 Sunwater, supplied 29 October 2026. Original CSV preserved here. User confirmed catchment and full-supply surface areas in hectares and full supply volume in megalitres. Surface area is at full supply; volume is a supplied full-supply attribute, not a live storage reading or verified current operating capacity. Coordinates and their unspecified datum remain unverified. No conversion from observed level to volume is attempted.

`python tools/build_dam_catalogue.py` builds `public/dams-catalogue.json` and the review list `nearest-water-gauges.json`. Gauges come from the existing audited mapped station catalogue. The nearest is chosen by great-circle distance to the supplied dam point, irrespective of water-plan boundaries or saved data availability. Identical-distance ties prefer a name without the TW token, then station ID. The available coordinates do not resolve such co-located headwater/tailwater sites physically. No name-based override of a genuinely closer point is applied. Distance in kilometres and the full station name are displayed, with storage association explicitly unverified. All mapped rainfall and water stations inside the matched water-plan polygon are drawn, plus the nearest water station even if outside the polygon. No full station list is rendered. The nearest water station opens by default; clicking another map point opens its own chart. Selection persists across time and duration changes, and resets to nearest water when a different dam is chosen.

This is not proof of storage-level measurement. The closest stations for **Eungella, Fred Haigh, Maroon and Wuruma** have TW in their BoM names. Bromelton, Isis, Moura and West Barron also select river gauges that may not measure the storage. No claim that most nearest gauges are storage gauges is made. A later verified association/override table may be appropriate. There is no silent switch to a different gauge if the nearest lacks observations.

## Water-plan areas

The original source extract is `data/dams/water-plan-source.zip`, including XML/HTML metadata and File Geodatabase. Dataset: Water plan catchments - Queensland; 187 valid polygons, all marked Current in the extract; EPSG:4283 GDA94. © The State of Queensland 2025, CC BY 4.0. Planning areas may follow cadastral rather than drainage boundaries, and are not verified upstream dam catchments.

46 dam points have a containing polygon; Burdekin Falls, EJ Beardmore and Leslie do not. The most specific Subcatchment Area is preferred to a parent Catchment Area, then smallest projected area. Both area title and broader water-plan name are displayed. No matching boundary is required for nearest-station selection. CatchmentBoundary remains null in the dam catalogue; contextual water-plan associations are separate.

Offline preparation: install pyogrio, shapely and pyproj, then `python tools/build_dam_boundaries.py`. Outputs contain source hash and matching report, plus 36 display outlines simplified 40 m in EPSG:3577 and transformed to EPSG:4326. Exact source-polygon gauge memberships control the additional map points. The nearest water station remains available independently of polygon membership. Rebuild after changing source dam/station inventory. No GIS packages are needed on Render.

## Watercourses

User-supplied `QSC_Extracted_Data_20261029_101604167654-3208.zip`: Watercourse identification map — watercourses, 9,311 source features in GDA2020 EPSG:7844. Source XML confirms © The State of Queensland 2025, CC BY 4.0. Original upload remains in the workspace attachments; this larger ZIP is not copied into Git. `watercourses-source.json` records its source filename, checksum and preparation settings.

Prepare with `python tools/build_dam_watercourses.py <source.zip>` using the same offline GIS dependencies. The dataset has **no stream order or primary/major classification**. Our display heuristic retains named rivers and named creeks with at least 10 km summed mapped length by exact name, excluding unnamed tributaries, drains and gullies. Shared names can refer to disconnected reaches: this is cartographic filtering, not a hydrological classification or complete river network. 4,059 source segments qualify statewide.

Features are clipped to each selected water-plan area; for the three unmatched dams, context is clipped to a 20 km radius. Display lines are simplified 30 m in Australian Albers, then converted to 2D EPSG:4326 GeoJSON. Source Z/M values are dropped for display only; they are not used as elevation or flow information. The dataset is a watercourse-identification map, not a guarantee of every stream's mapping or physical extent. One per-dam context file loads on selection. Toggle hides/shows streams without changing observations, map position or ending time; hover identifies the stream. Attribution remains visible.

## Gauge observations and links

Only the basin frames needed for the precomputed in-area station IDs and nearest water station are queried, at most two concurrently. Water and rainfall extents are independent; a failed layer does not discard successful layers. Station series are requested on selection. Missing frame values remain grey; they are not treated as zero. No new database tables or reading writes are introduced. Default duration 24 h, up to 7 d. Ending time is Brisbane AEST; Latest preserves the exact saved water timestamp, including seconds, while manual ending entry uses minute resolution. Paused selections stay paused across duration changes. Water timing does not depend on rainfall availability. Rainfall frame/series times are rounded down to quarter hours and bounded by its saved extent, with the rainfall ending time displayed explicitly. Outside the rainfall saved range, points remain grey and charts state that readings are unavailable. The hydrograph opens automatically after frame loading; explicit gauge clicks can scroll phones to it. Stale async responses are ignored. Boundary or stream loading failure does not block the gauge.

Measured water levels remain at original observation times. Missing readings and datum changes break the line; measured endpoints more than 60 minutes apart are joined by dashed segments, without creating intermediate readings. No interpolated levels, rainfall estimates, runoff, storage response or warnings are generated. Current display colours use the existing measured 60-minute movement comparison. Normal document scrolling works over the map without wheel zoom.

BoM plot/table paths are taken from the station catalogue or existing verified-link API. Paths must match the selected six-digit station ID and expected BoM path pattern; no guessed direct plot URLs. Where a direct link is unavailable the source group bulletin remains available when present. The full Water levels viewer link carries station, basin, ending and duration. Direct BoM links show its current record, potentially different dates from the selected saved window.

## Viewer refinements

The three numeric reference-stat titles are **Assumed surface area at full supply**, **Assumed full supply volume**, and **Assumed catchment area**. Owner and water-plan names retain their descriptive titles.

The card below the automatic water hydrograph lists the **closest rainfall gauge**, its distance to the dam and a link to the Rainfall viewer. This replaces the redundant water-gauge list; the map shows in-area gauges, with the nearest water-level gauge as the default plot. Rainfall association uses the same geographic distance method and mapped station catalogue, without implying catchment representation. The link carries the duration and selected ending time rounded down to a measured rainfall quarter-hour; Rainfall applies its existing archive-extent checks. Rainfall frames support the in-area map points; a rainfall series is requested only when its point is selected. Water plots remain lines, rainfall plots use the same chart-interval aggregation, complete-period rules and 24-hour IFD comparison as the main Rainfall viewer, with gaps for missing values.

Neutral grey dam-name labels appear from zoom 11, next to visible dam pins, with the selected dam prioritised. Blue italic watercourse labels appear only from zoom 13. Stream text rotates along a smoothed local path tangent and remains upright/readable; the visible line must be at least 80 pixels and long enough for its label. Names are deduplicated and rotated bounding boxes checked against dam labels and earlier stream labels. Limits are 12 dam names and 12 watercourse names (6 of each on narrow maps). Labels refresh after pan, zoom, resize or dam changes; disabling Named watercourses hides its lines and names while dam names remain. Labels are noninteractive. Names come from the uploaded source data; label priority is cartographic, not a stream hierarchy.


## Dam-centred zoom and Somerset point adjustment

Selecting a dam fits a symmetric extent around that dam, large enough to include its contextual boundary and nearest water station. Zoom buttons, keyboard zoom and pointer-based double-click zoom remain anchored to the selected point. Phone pinch uses centre-based zoom and a final recenter guard. Users can still pan deliberately; subsequent zoom returns to the selected dam. Wheel zoom remains disabled for page scrolling. Map-center anchoring is local to Dams and does not change the separate rainfall/water viewers.

The original Somerset location (-27.11556, 152.55667) is approximately 1.7 m outside the Stanley River water-plan polygon, in Central Brisbane River; the Upper Brisbane River polygon is approximately 8.2 km away. At the user's request, its map point was shifted 31.7 m to (-27.115388375, 152.556927099), about 30 m inside the original Stanley polygon. This point also lies within the simplified display polygon. This is an explicit contextual map adjustment, not a surveyed correction to the physical dam location.

`data/dams/location-adjustments.json` records the original and adjusted coordinates, reason and distance. The original CSV is unchanged. The catalogue builder validates that the original source coordinates still match before applying the adjustment, retains the original coordinates in the public record and then recomputes nearest stations. The water-plan index, gauge membership and Somerset watercourse context were rebuilt from the adjusted point. Its nearest water station remains 040820, Stanley River at Somerset Dam.


## Shared plots across viewers

`public/gauge-plots.js` is the single implementation of water hydrographs and rainfall hyetographs in Dams and the main viewers. Identical gauge, selected ending and duration therefore produce identical measured datasets, axes, gap rules, default point settings, chart sizes, interval controls and rainfall totals. Water plots show every datum as a separate dataset; they do not silently discard older datums. Recorded points default off and the toggle persists within each page across selections. Data notes and measured comparison details match the main viewer.

Rainfall uses the selected duration for its hyetograph, with independent chart intervals through one day. Every bar requires all its constituent valid nonnegative quarter-hours; missing is not zero. Selected-duration and 24-hour totals are separate metrics. `public/ifd-view.js` now exports an isolated reusable GaugeIFD view; both pages use the same local dataset/scenario selection, reference fallback labelling, no-marker curves and preceding-24-hour wettest-window logic. Choosing a chart interval does not change IFD calculations. Switching away destroys the IFD chart and clears its observation context so late reference loads cannot revive a previous gauge.

Comparison tests feed the same observations into both viewers and compare chart datasets, multiple datums, long-gap styling, null readings, timestamp seconds, point toggles, chart heights, incomplete rain bars, interval selection and seven-day charts with unchanged 24-hour IFD bounds. No collection or database-writing behavior changes.
